HTML Symbol Reference

1. Reserved Characters: Use < for the less-than symbol, > for the greater-than symbol, and & for the ampersand symbol in HTML.

2. Currency Symbols: Prices can be displayed as €99, £45, and ¥5000 using HTML entities.

3. Mathematical Symbols: Example: 10 < 20, 25 > 15, and 8 × 5 = 40.

4. Arrows: Navigation links can use ← Back, → Next, and ↑ Top.

5. Emojis and Legal Symbols: Made with ❤ for developers, protected by © 2026, and approved ✅ for quality.
